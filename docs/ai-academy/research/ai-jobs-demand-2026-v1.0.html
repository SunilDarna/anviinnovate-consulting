Bottom line. AI hiring is outgrowing every other job category on every authoritative index. PwC finds AI-specialist postings grew ~69% in a single year (~8× the overall market) and command a ~62% wage premium. LinkedIn ranks AI Engineer as the #1 fastest-growing U.S. job. India's Naukri JobSpeak shows AI/ML hiring +45% for FY26 while the broader IT sector stayed flat. Through 2030 the WEF projects a net +78M jobs globally, with AI reshaping 86% of businesses. Demand stays supply-constrained through at least 2027 — most acute in India (projected 1.5–2× talent shortfall).

Net read: demand stays supply-constrained through at least 2027 — most acute in India — with value shifting toward applied GenAI (RAG, agents, fine-tuning), MLOps and evaluation, and toward people who can verify AI output. Main risks to these numbers: entry-level exposure to automation, and salary compression in the hottest specializations once supply catches up post-2027.

4 · Confidence & caveats

Strongest evidence: LinkedIn Jobs on the Rise 2026, PwC AI Jobs Barometer 2026 (1B+ ads, 27 countries), WEF Future of Jobs 2025 (1,000+ employers), US BLS, Naukri JobSpeak — all primary and cross-corroborated (3-vote adversarial verification).

Weaker — attribute explicitly: India's "+32% / 380K roles" rests on a single foundit report; salary bands come from recruiter/course-provider sources (rescued by independent corroboration, split verification votes); the 2.3M-openings and 21%/yr figures are consulting-firm model estimates, not census data. Hiring indices are monthly snapshots and salary bands move fast in a hot market; all 2027–2030 figures are projections.
