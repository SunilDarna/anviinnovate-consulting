AI Talent Academy — Zero-to-Hero Curriculum

An end-to-end training program that takes fresh graduates and 3rd/4th-year students from foundations to job-ready for the most in-demand 2026 AI roles — a shared foundations track that branches into six role specializations, with labs, assessments, capstones and portfolio projects.

Compiled Aug 2026 · grounded in DeepLearning.AI · roadmap.sh · Scaler · Dataquest · UPenn Career Services · IBM/FourthBrain capstones · NASSCOM-Deloitte "Advancing India's AI Skills"
Program blueprint Role tracks
The opportunity. Per the NASSCOM-Deloitte "Advancing India's AI Skills" report, India's AI talent demand roughly doubles from ~600,000–650,000 (2022) to over 1.25 million by 2027 (~15% CAGR) while the AI software/services market grows 25–35% CAGR — a clear demand-supply gap a training company can fill (Bain frames it as ~2.3M openings vs ~1.2M talent by 2027). The design principle (validated by two independent industry roadmaps): a shared foundations track, then role branches, sequenced foundations → classical ML → deep learning → GenAI/LLMs/RAG → MLOps/deployment → capstone. "Job-ready" is defined as able to apply with a portfolio to show — not expert-level — so every track ends in a documented, end-to-end capstone plus a multi-project portfolio.

1 · Program blueprint

Two phases everyone shares (Foundations + Core AI), then a role specialization, then MLOps + capstone. Full-time = 6 months (~24 weeks, ~40 h/wk); part-time = 9–12 months (10–15 h/wk). Each phase is a hard prerequisite for the next.

4 · Role specialization tracks (Phase E · Weeks 19–22)

Students pick one. Each adds role-specific depth on top of the shared spine, a role-specific tech stack, and a role capstone. AI Engineer is the anchor (highest 2026 demand and the most thoroughly evidenced); Solutions Architect is an advanced track for stronger grads.

Scope
Apply pre-trained models & tools to build production AI features — not build models from scratch (roadmap.sh; model-training in only ~6% of AI-Engineer JDs).

RAG optimization, agents & tool use, guardrails (refusal logic, output validation, escalation), evals/observability, inference cost & latency, model routing, deployment.

5 · Assessment & checkpoint framework

Every module is graded on three instruments; every phase has a checkpoint; the program ends with a capstone defense. Capstones are a mandatory, documented, end-to-end graduation requirement (FourthBrain/IBM model).

LevelInstrumentWhat it measures
ModuleAuto-graded quiz (concepts) + coding challenge (skills) + lab rubricKnowledge recall + applied skill
Phase checkpointGraded project against a rubric; ≥70–80% to advanceIntegration of the phase's skills
CapstoneRubric: problem framing · correctness · eval rigor · code quality · docs · deployment · presentationEnd-to-end shipping capability
Exit / job-readyPortfolio review + mock interview (role-specific: RAG/agent design, evals, cost/latency)Market readiness
Concept bank for AI-Engineer assessments (from 2026 interview analysis): RAG architecture · embeddings & semantic similarity · tokenization/token-counting · temperature/calibration · quantization trade-offs · context windows · continuous batching · KV-cache reuse · GPU utilization · LLM-as-a-judge evaluation.

8 · Confidence, caveats & sources

Evidence strength: 24 of 25 researched claims confirmed via 3-vote adversarial verification. Market sizing and course benchmarks rest on primary sources (NASSCOM-Deloitte, DeepLearning.AI, IBM/Coursera, government portals). Sequencing and tech-stack specifics draw on reputable edtech roadmaps (Scaler, Dataquest) and roadmap.sh — industry convention, heavily cross-corroborated, but not a single canonical standard.

Caveats: the AI Engineer track is the most thoroughly evidenced; the other five tracks apply the same validated structure/stack with role-specific design (synthesis). The 2026 tech stack (LangChain/LangGraph/CrewAI, vector DBs, inference techniques) and interview focus drift fast — re-check before each cohort.
