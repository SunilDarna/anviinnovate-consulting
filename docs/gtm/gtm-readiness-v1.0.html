The verdict

Do not launch the site as it stands. Not because the idea is wrong — the model is aimed at a real and growing demand pocket — but because three things on the live site would actively cost you credibility with the exact buyer you are targeting, and two structural risks are unpriced.
What is right. The buyer you have implicitly chosen is the correct one. GCCs are 68–71% of revenue at both listed Indian staffing leaders, and Hyderabad is a genuine GCC market. AI/ML is the fastest-growing hiring category in India. The technical depth of the role tracks is real and current, which most staffing competitors cannot match.
What is unresolved. Your supply is freshers. GCC fresher share is falling (21% → 16% of planned hiring), and India's largest IT employers have cut fresher intake hard. The evidence does not say freshers are unsellable — it says they are unsellable as freshers. The offer has to be restated around what the buyer is short of, which is 1–5 year AI-capable engineers.

What is wrong on the live site today

These are specific, checkable, and fixable this week.

P0 The only market statistic you cite describes the wrong market.

Both the homepage and /about cite Grand View Research's global AI training dataset market — USD 8.60bn by 2030, 21.9% CAGR. That is the data-annotation market: labelling images and text. It is Scale AI's and iMerit's market, not yours. It is also global, and smaller than India's staffing market alone, so it simultaneously misidentifies your category and understates your opportunity. A GCC procurement reader who checks it concludes you have mis-read your own market.

P0 Your positioning line is one word from a far larger competitor's.

Verified live on 7 Oct 2026 — Andela's H1 is "The Human Layer Powering Production AI". Yours is "The skilled human layer behind AI". Entering a category on a funded competitor's distinctive phrase forfeits recall at precisely the moment a buyer is forming a shortlist.

P0 "Deploy in days, not months" is unsourced, and you have better material.

TCS has publicly stated freshers need roughly nine months of training before project work. That is a citable third-party anchor that makes your claim concrete instead of a vendor assertion. Restate it as a two-part, defensible commitment — shortlist within N hours from the bench, onboarded in N weeks after the client's own interview — and back it with a written replacement clause.

P1 Nine role tracks reads as having none.

India's largest staffing firm sustains its entire niche contract business on roughly 7,129 associates with about 1,100 open mandates at any time. Nine tracks off a single pre-trained bench is not credible at your scale, and the shortfall buyers actually report is concentrated in AI/ML operations, data, and platform engineering.

P1 Zero first-party numbers, where competitors lead with them.

Revature leads with 25,000+ trained and 200+ enterprises; Andela with 17K engineers and 200K trained. You need no clients to publish: people trained, bench headcount by track, lab hours, median days from request to shortlist, assessment pass rates. All first-party, all defensible.

How enterprises and GCCs actually buy contract tech talent

At large Indian enterprises, buying contract IT talent is a two-stage process: you must first win empanelment through an open EOI, after which actual work is awarded only in closed tenders among the already-empanelled pool.high
SBI's RFP for procurement of IT-Human Resources states on its cover: "This is a closed RFP and participation in this RFP is by invitation only." Eligibility criterion 1 is simply "The Bidder should be the Empaneled Bidder under the Category III pursuant to the EOI", evidenced by "Copy of the Empanelment Letter issued by the Bank". A non-empanelled vendor cannot bid at all, regardless of capability or price.
State Bank of India (RFP dated 01.11.2025, for contract 1 Jan 2026 – 31 Dec 2027) · RFP issued 1 November 2025; contract period 1 Jan 2026 – 31 Dec 2027 · source
Once inside the empanelled pool, IT staffing work is awarded by online reverse auction to the lowest bidder (L1) — not on speed, quality or bench readiness.high
The same SBI RFP schedules a "Reverse Auction" as a listed event, and states "The Bidder will be selected as L1 on the basis of net total of the price evaluation as quoted in the Reverse Auction" and "Among all qualified bids, the lowest bid (as quoted in reverse auction) will be termed as L1." Shortlisted bidders must agree to participate. Speed-to-deploy is not a scored commercial variable at this stage.
State Bank of India (closed RFP / limited tender enquiry for IT-Human Resources) · 1 November 2025 · source
Empanelment is a long-lived licence, awarded in windows that open only every 2–5 years — miss the window and there is no route in.high
IDBI: "The empanelment of the selected vendors is for five years from date of signing of LOI", extendable by one further year, with yearly performance review and possible de-empanelment. India and Telangana licensing and labour compliance for manpower supply

The legal basis of India's contract staffing industry changed on 21 November 2025: all four Labour Codes were brought into force and the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act 1970 was repealed into the OSH Code 2020.high
The Government notified the Code on Wages 2019, Industrial Relations Code 2020, Code on Social Security 2020 and Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code 2020 on 21 November 2025, consolidating 29 central labour laws. CLRA 1970 and its 1971 Central Rules are subsumed and superseded. Any compliance page, client MSA or legal annexure on anviinnovate.com that cites 'CLRA licence' as the governing instrument is now out of date.
DLA Piper Global Employment Latest Developments · Codes in force 21 November 2025 · source
The contract-labour licensing threshold doubled from 20 to 50 workers, so Anvi needs no contractor licence until it has 50 or more deployed contract workers on any day in the preceding 12 months.high
Under the OSH Code the contract-labour chapter applies to establishments engaging 50 or more contract labour, and to every contractor (including a pure manpower-supply contractor) who employed 50 or more contract labour on any day of the preceding 12 months. Below that, no contractor licence is required. For a pre-revenue bench business this is a genuine 12-24 month runway before the licence becomes a gating item — but it also means the moment the 50th deployment happens, an unlicensed supply becomes an offence.
Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan · OSH Code in force 21 November 2025 · source
'Contractor' under the OSH Code now expressly includes a person who supplies manpower as a mere human resource — closing the argument that pure bench-deployment is outside the contract-labour regime.high
The OSH Code definition of contractor covers supply of manpower as a mere human resource, which was absent from CLRA 1970. The definition of contract labour was also widened to include workers in supervisory roles earning up to INR 18,000 per month (up from INR 500) and inter-state migrant workers. Anvi's 'deploy from a pre-trained bench' model is squarely a manpower-supply contract, not a services contract, unless deliberately restructured.
Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan · OSH Code in force 21 November 2025 · source
Section 57 of the OSH Code prohibits contract labour in a client's core activity, with only three narrow exceptions — this is the single largest legal threat to Anvi's stated model of deploying engineers into client engineering teams.high
Core activity means any activity for which the establishment is set up, including any activity essential or necessary to it. The proviso to s.57(1) permits contract labour in core activity only where (a) the activity is ordinarily done through a contractor in the normal functioning of the establishment, (b) the activity does not require full-time workers for the major portion of working hours, or (c) there is a sudden increase in volume of work to be completed in a specified time. Non-core exceptions listed are sanitation, watch and ward, courier, housekeeping, laundry and transport. For a software product or services client, engineering is the core activity — so Anvi's placements must be justified under (a), (b) or (c), or structured as something other than contract labour supply.
Simpliance / Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan · OSH Code s.57, in force 21 November 2025 · source
Relabelling a staffing arrangement as 'managed services' does not defeat s.57 if the deployed engineers remain functionally embedded in the client's team.medium
Legal commentary on the core-activity boundary states that merely relabelling a manpower supply arrangement as a managed services contract will not reduce risk if vendor personnel remain functionally embedded in the principal employer's core operations; classification turns on functional integration, not contractual designation. The test examines the purpose for which the client establishment was set up and whether the activity is essential to it. Anvi's own copy — 'deploy into client engineering teams' — is an admission of embedding.
Mondaq (Indian law firm commentary on outsourcing and the core-activity boundary) · 2026 commentary on codes in force from 21 November 2025 · source
Software engineers are not outside the labour regime: Indian courts have held that an engineer in a software company doing skilled technical work is a 'workman'.medium
A labour court held that the job of an engineer in a software company involved skills and technical knowledge and so the engineer was a workman; 'worker' definitions across the ID Act and the Codes cover manual, skilled, unskilled, technical, operational, clerical and supervisory work. The IT sector's long-held assumption that it sits outside industrial labour law has been eroded. Anvi cannot rely on 'these are white-collar AI engineers, not workers' as a compliance position.

The central rules completing the new regime were notified on 8 May 2026 — OSH (Central) Rules 2026 via G.S.R. 345(E) — with Shram Suvidha as the single registration and licensing portal.high
On 8 May 2026 the Government notified the Code on Wages (Central) Rules 2026, Social Security (Central) Rules 2026, OSH (Central) Rules 2026 and Industrial Relations (Central) Rules 2026. OSH Rules provide a single registration through the Shram Suvidha Portal with establishments deemed registered within 7 days of a complete application and an auto-generated certificate. Contractors apply for licence electronically; the licence is valid 5 years (replacing annual CLRA licences), specifies the number of contract labour that may be supplied, and a contractor may hold one licence for more than one state or for the whole of India. A 'work specific licence' can be issued for a particular work order.
Sansa Legal / KPMG GMS Flash Alert 2026-127 · notified 8 May 2026 · source
Contractor licensing economics are modest: security deposit of INR 1,000 per contract labour, 5-year validity, renewal 90-30 days before expiry with a 25% late fee.medium
OSH Rules 2026 set security at INR 1,000 per contract labour, with fixed caps for very large contractors (INR 10 crore for 1-1.5 lakh workers, INR 15 crore for 1.5-2 lakh, INR 20 crore for 2 lakh+). Renewal applications are accepted 90 to 30 days before expiry, delayed applications attract a 25% additional fee, and the licensing authority must renew within 7 days. Contractor licence application is Form XXI on Shram Suvidha; the principal employer annual return is Form XVII (Part III). For a 100-person bench the deposit is roughly INR 1 lakh — not a barrier, so there is no cost excuse for operating unlicensed past 50.

Telangana has NOT yet finalised its state rules under three of the four Codes — all are still at draft stage as of late 2026 — so Hyderabad-sphere staffing compliance runs on legacy machinery plus the Codes' repeal-and-savings clauses.medium
Telangana's Labour, Employment, Training and Factories Department published the draft Telangana OSH Rules 2026 on 8 May 2026 (45 days for objections), the draft Code on Social Security (Telangana) Rules 2026 on 24 June 2026, and the draft Industrial Relations (Telangana State) Rules 2026 on 2 February 2026. GST, contracting and data protection obligations

Contract staffing and manpower supply attract 18% GST with full input tax credit, under the SAC 9985 'support services' family (998511 executive search, 998512 permanent placement, 998513 contract staffing, 998514 temporary staffing, 998515 long-term staffing/payroll).medium
All of Anvi's sellable motions - contract staffing, contract-to-hire and hire-train-deploy - fall in SAC 9985 at the 18% standard rate, not a concessional slab. The SAC sub-code matters for invoice hygiene and for how clients book the cost: 998513 is the right code when the engineer stays on Anvi's rolls, 998512/998511 when Anvi bills a one-time placement fee. The commonly cited invoicing practice is to show headcount, days/hours and the SAC on the face of the invoice.
Busy (GST software vendor reference pages) · Rates current as at 2026; SAC structure unchanged since 2017 · source
The 22 September 2025 GST rate rationalisation collapsed the slabs to 5% / 18% / 40% but left staffing services at the 18% standard rate.high
The 56th GST Council meeting (3 September 2025) recommended the two-rate structure, given effect by Notification No. 15/2025-Central Tax (Rate) and companion notifications amending Notification 11/2017-CTR, effective 22 September 2025. The reductions targeted consumer goods, agri-equipment and household electronics; business support and manpower services stayed at 18%. There is no pending relief for staffing, so Anvi should not price on any assumption of a future rate cut.
EY India / CBIC notifications alert; Tally Solutions · Effective 22 September 2025 · source
GST is payable on the ENTIRE invoice value of a manpower supply - including the deployed engineer's salary and the employer's EPF and ESI contributions. Staffing firms are not 'pure agents'.high
The Telangana AAR (Bhagyalakshmi Devamma Vangimallu, TSAAR Order No. 14/2021) and the West Bengal AAR both held that Section 15(2) CGST leaves no room to deduct reimbursed salary, EPF or ESI from taxable value, and that a manpower supplier is not a pure agent under Rule 33. Consequence for Anvi's pricing: an engineer costing INR 1,00,000/month billed at a 30% markup is invoiced at INR 1,30,000 + INR 23,400 GST, not GST on the INR 30,000 margin. Any client who is GST-exempt or has blocked credit (hospitals, education, some BFSI, government bodies) bears that 18% as a real cost, which materially changes Anvi's competitiveness in those segments.
GST Council (AAR order, primary) and VJM Global · TSAAR Order 14/2021; West Bengal AAR line of rulings 2019-2024 · source
There is no reverse charge on general manpower supply. RCM applies only to supply of SECURITY PERSONNEL, and only where the supplier is not a body corporate.high
Notification No. 29/2018-Central Tax (Rate) dated 31 December 2018, effective 1 January 2019, put security personnel supply (SAC 998521) under RCM at 18% when supplied to a registered person by any person other than a body corporate. Two implications for Anvi: (a) as a company, Anvi always charges GST under forward charge on its own staffing invoices, including any security-adjacent deployment - a client claiming 'we'll pay under RCM' is wrong; (b) if Anvi sources bench or sub-vendor manpower for security roles from a proprietorship or partnership, Anvi becomes liable to self-assess RCM GST and cannot net it off the sub-vendor invoice.
